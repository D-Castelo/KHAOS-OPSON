# Preparation Process

## Experiment 001 — Attempt 001

### Method

Fried egg — Sunny-Side Up.

### Preparation Sequence

1. Prepare the frying pan and utensils.
2. Heat the pan.
3. Add the oil.
4. Crack the egg into a separate bowl.
5. Transfer the egg into the pan.
6. Allow the egg white to cook.
7. Use the cooking fat to help finish the egg white.
8. Cover the pan briefly to help cook the upper portion.
9. Remove the egg and serve.

### Variables

| Variable | Recorded Value |
|---|---|
| Heat intensity | Low-Medium |
| Cooking time | More or less 1 minute |
