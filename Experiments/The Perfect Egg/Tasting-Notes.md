# Tasting Notes

## Experiment 001 — Attempt 001

### First Impression

The egg was tasty; a quite pleasant initial result.

### Experience with the Yolk

The yolk was almost entirely liquid, featuring a thin, slightly firmer outer layer.

Upon breaking through this layer, there was a slight resistance before the yolk began to flow.

The interior had a rich, liquid consistency.

### Overall Experience

A delicious egg, though imperfect in both appearance and flavor—yet, the moment the yolk broke open, it was as if a river of gold were flowing across the plate and the egg itself... it was beautiful.
