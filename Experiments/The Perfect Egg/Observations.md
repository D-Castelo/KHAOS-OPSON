# Technical Observations

## Experiment 001 — Attempt 001

### Visual Appearance

- Yolk remained intact.
- Egg white had an irregular shape.
- Edges developed a golden coloration, with some darker areas.
- Overall appearance was promising, but uneven.

### Texture

- Yolk was almost entirely liquid.
- A thin, slightly firmer surface layer formed over the yolk.
- The yolk offered slight resistance before breaking.

### Technical Observations

The darker and uneven edges may indicate that the cooking temperature was higher than ideal or unevenly distributed.

The surface of the yolk appeared to have coagulated slightly while its interior remained liquid.

### Potential Issues

1. Uneven cooking around the egg white.
2. Irregular shape.
3. Some edges became darker than desired.
